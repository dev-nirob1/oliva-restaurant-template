Discover our story

Oliva opened in 2012 as a small family kitchen with eight tables and one wood oven. Today we cook for hundreds of guests every week, but our approach has not changed: buy well, cook simply and treat every guest like a neighbour.

Our menu follows the seasons, our bread is baked every morning and every sauce is made from scratch.
